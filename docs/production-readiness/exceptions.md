# Exceptions

Human-approved exceptions for Critical/High findings that cannot be fixed within their phase
(plan §4.6, §5.2). An exception is written here as `PENDING` with risk, blast radius, mitigation and
expiry, and execution stops until the owner approves or rejects it.

| ID | Finding | Risk | Blast radius | Mitigation | Expiry | Status | Approved by / date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
