2. **D2 — High: registration, startup and shutdown do not atomically own subscriptions.**  
   [EventHandlerRegistrar.java:143](C:/Users/Ali/AppData/Local/Temp/hd/codex-p4r1/messaging/platform-messaging-autoconfigure/src/main/java/ae/gov/dubaicustoms/platform/messaging/autoconfigure/internal/EventHandlerRegistrar.java:143), with `start()` at 301 and shutdown snapshot/clear at 338–345. Concrete interleavings:
   
   - Registration observes `running=true`, enters `subscribe()`, and blocks. `stop()` closes its snapshot and returns. Subscription creation then completes and adds a live consumer to a stopped registrar.
   - A subscription added between the shutdown snapshot and `subscriptions.clear()` is erased without being closed.
   - Registration subscribes a new destination after `start()` sets `running`, then startup’s key iteration subscribes it again.
   
   These produce untracked consumers, consumption after shutdown, or duplicate subscriptions. The short synchronized sections do not cover the transport operation or shutdown’s snapshot.

3. **D3 — High: the outbox can drain before in-memory subscriptions exist.**  
   [EventHandlerRegistrar.java:316](C:/Users/Ali/AppData/Local/Temp/hd/codex-p4r1/messaging/platform-messaging-autoconfigure/src/main/java/ae/gov/dubaicustoms/platform/messaging/autoconfigure/internal/EventHandlerRegistrar.java:316). 4. **D4 — Medium: restart replaces the cancellation signal still needed by old deliveries.**  
   [EventHandlerRegistrar.java:286](C:/Users/Ali/AppData/Local/Temp/hd/codex-p4r1/messaging/platform-messaging-autoconfigure/src/main/java/ae/gov/dubaicustoms/platform/messaging/autoconfigure/internal/EventHandlerRegistrar.java:286), replacement at 307.
